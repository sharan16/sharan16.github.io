Lines suggest a route toward a harder variation. A fork offers alternatives, not a requirement to complete both. Paths are curated guides, not automatic readiness assessments.

“Next” is a nearby unmarked stage after your current focus or furthest marked stage. You can explore or mark any exercise at any time. Earlier stages are never filled in automatically.

Atlas totals count explicitly marked variations across this library. They are not a percentage of your strength or fitness.
