How the tree works

Green means Can do. Orange means Working on. Empty circles are unmarked, not locked.

Within a skill path, downward lines suggest harder variations. Forks offer alternatives—you do not need both. Marking a skill does not mark earlier skills for you.

Totals count skills you explicitly marked, not your fitness level. Your data saves locally and syncs directly to your personal JSONBin.
